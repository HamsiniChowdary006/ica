## 1. AI regulation rather than ban is better option: CJI
**Source:** Bing News · **Published:** 2026-11-21T06:33:00+00:00  
**Link:** https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/telangana/ai-regulation-rather-than-ban-is-better-option-cji/article71728733.ece

Setting out clear rules to regulate usage of AI tools rather than a total embargo would be the right option for legal students and profession, said Chief Justice of India Justice Surya Kant. Delivering the inaugural address at the first annual international conference on legal education convened by the Commonwealth Legal Education Association here on Saturday (November 21), the CJI observed that generative artificial intelligence had unsettled the assumption that an answer sheet reflects the knowledge of a student. Depending on AI tools for legal solutions without undergoing these rigorous training of draftings and legal reasonings, the students might lose the capacity to analyse and fail to distinguish between the right and the wrong, he said.
